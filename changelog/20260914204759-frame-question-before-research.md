# Frame the question before research in deep-research-answer

- Replaced Step 0 "Question preparation" with "Question framing". It extracts the goal, the constraints, and the unknowns, generates alternative angles, selects a primary question plus 3 to 6 sub-questions, and prints the full query list before research starts.
- Threaded the frame through the verdict line, both research passes, and the reasoning step. The source floor stays on the primary question. Each sub-question needs one primary-source finding or is marked unverified.
- Split the output into `Quick answer` and `Deep dive` blocks with concision rules that cut prose, never a fact or a number.
- Why: the skill answered the literal wording and missed the asker's real goal, so the verdict was correct but useless.
