# Add a stance lens to indie-hacker-wrapup

- Added Lens C (stance) to `indie-hacker-wrapup`, so the scheduled X draft job mines decisions (kills, parks, rejected options, pushback) instead of incident reports.
- Stance angles that clear the bar rank first. Stance posts open on the decision, and every post ends on a fact or a number, not a maxim.
- Bumped the skill to version 1.1.
- Why: four nightly drafts were rejected as incident reports that ended on stock maxims.
