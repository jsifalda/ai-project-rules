# Landing copy skills catch competitor-first and jargon heroes

- Added a rule to `landing-page-copy` and `landing-page-viral-grill` that bans another product's name in the hero and meta, except on a "<competitor> alternative" page.
- Added a cold-reader check: the H1 and subhead alone must say what it is, who it is for and why act now, with no unknown tool names or acronyms.
- `landing-page-copy` now rejects a `one_line_pitch` that is a feature list or a competitor framing.
- Fixed a contradiction: a technical ICP adds specs in Features only. The hero, headings and meta stay benefit-first with zero jargon.
- Why: a real home page shipped a competitor-named, feature-first hero that a buyer outside the category could not parse. The rules did not cover it.
