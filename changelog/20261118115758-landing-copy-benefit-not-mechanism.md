# Landing copy skills cut mechanism copy outside the hero

- Added a translate-or-cut rule to `landing-page-copy`: every interval, internal count, score or internal object name becomes the visitor's outcome, or goes. It covers body, stats, FAQ, CTAs, captions and mockup labels. Comparison and setup content is exempt.
- Added an audit pass that lists every number, stat, mockup label, FAQ answer and CTA with its buyer benefit, plus scored anti-patterns for internal stats and internal-object CTAs.
- Added a brief rule: a mechanism detail locked in a brief is a question for the owner, never copy.
- Aligned the Features, FAQ and numbers rules so they no longer reward mechanism lines. `landing-page-viral-grill` gained matching checks.
- Why: a page passed the hero gates and still showed a polling interval, internal check counts, scores and a CTA that named an internal object.
